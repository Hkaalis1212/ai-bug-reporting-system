# Case Study: Regional Freight Carrier Saves $31,000 Annually

**Client:** Mid-size freight carrier, 15 trucks, Southeast routes
**Challenge:** Rising fuel costs eating into already thin margins
**Timeline:** 72-hour analysis and report delivery

## The Situation

A regional freight carrier operating 15 trucks was struggling with:
- Inconsistent fuel efficiency across the fleet
- Rising diesel costs impacting profitability
- Lack of visibility into which trucks/drivers were underperforming
- No clear strategy for improvement

## The Analysis

Using our Fleet Optimization Audit process:

1. **Data Collection:** Client exported 6 months of trip data (500+ records)
2. **Analysis:** Comprehensive review of fuel efficiency, route patterns, driver performance
3. **Opportunity Identification:** Three major areas for improvement identified

## The Findings

### 1. Vehicle Performance Issues ($15,000 annual impact)
- 3 trucks performing significantly below fleet average
- Average MPG: 4.8 vs fleet average of 6.2
- **Root Cause:** Deferred maintenance and engine issues
- **Recommendation:** Immediate maintenance schedule + potential replacement analysis

### 2. Route Optimization ($8,200 annual impact)  
- Analysis revealed inefficient routing patterns
- Some routes 12% longer than optimal
- **Recommendation:** Route planning software implementation

### 3. Driver Training Opportunity ($7,800 annual impact)
- Top 20% of drivers averaged 15% better fuel efficiency
- **Recommendation:** Fuel-efficient driving training program

## The Results

**Total Annual Savings Identified:** $31,000
**Implementation Cost:** $8,500
**Net Annual Benefit:** $22,500
**Payback Period:** 4.1 months
